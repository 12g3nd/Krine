About Krine

Krine is a safe space for anonymous reflections. We believe that honesty thrives when identity is removed from the equation. Our platform is designed to let you express your true thoughts without the pressure of a persona.

Built with privacy at its core, Krine uses advanced AI moderation to keep the community safe while maintaining your anonymity.

{% elif page_name == 'mission' %}

Our Mission

To connect humanity through shared, unfiltered, and anonymous experiences.

We strive to build a corner of the internet where you can be yourself—completely unmasked. We value:

  • Anonymity: Your identity is your own.
  • Safety: A toxic-free environment powered by smart moderation.
  • Simplicity: No distractions, just thoughts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it really anonymous?

Yes. We do not require account creation. We do not track your IP for marketing purposes. We use basic session IDs only to prevent spam and handle likes, but these are transient.

How do you moderate content?

We use a locally running AI model (DistilBERT) to scan every post for toxicity, hate speech, and inappropriate content before it goes live.
