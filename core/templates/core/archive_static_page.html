What this archive keeps

Public posts that had completed moderation and were not flagged, their public tags, public comments, recorded like totals, timestamps, and media attached to those surviving posts.

What it deliberately leaves behind

Browser sessions, write endpoints, AI model files, moderation workers, server logs, private environment variables, database credentials, and moderation-only records are not part of the public artifact.

How it behaves now

Search, sorting, and type filters run locally in your browser. Entry pages are ordinary static files. There is no application server listening behind the interface and nothing you do here is written back anywhere.

Source

The original application and the archive tooling remain visible in the Krine source repository.

About Krine

Krine is a safe haven for anonymous words. It exists as a thought experiment due to our key belief that honesty thrives when identity is removed from the equation entirely. Our platform is designed to let you express your true thoughts without the pressure of a persona or a mask.

Built with privacy at its very core, Krine uses advanced moderation to keep the community safe while maintaining your anonymity. Write whatever comes to mind and see what others write.

{% elif page_name == 'mission' %}

Our Mission

To connect humanity through shared, unfiltered, and anonymous experiences.

We strive to carve out a small niche within the internet where you can be yourself. We pride ourselves on three key values: anonymity, safety, and simplicity. Krine is a place where you are not afraid and not alone.

{% elif page_name == 'faq' %}

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it really anonymous?

Yes. We do not require account creation. We do not track your IP for marketing purposes. We use basic session IDs only to prevent spam and handle likes, but these are transient.

How do you moderate content?

We use a smart Zero-Shot AI model to analyze every post. It intelligently assigns emotional "vibes" and filters unsafe content.

{% elif page_name == 'legal' %}

Terms & Privacy

Terms of Service

By accessing specific features of Krine, you agree to these terms. Krine is a platform for anonymous expression, but it is not a lawless zone.

  • Respect: Hate speech, bullying, and harassment are strictly prohibited.
  • Legality: Content promoting illegal acts, violence, or self-harm will be removed immediately.
  • Rights: You retain ownership of your words, but you grant Krine a license to display them anonymously.

Privacy Policy

Our privacy philosophy is simple: We cannot lose data we do not have.

  • No Accounts: We do not ask for your email, name, or phone number.
  • No Tracking: We do not use third-party analytics or tracking pixels.
  • Transient Data: Session IDs are used solely to prevent spam and allow for interaction within a single browser session.
{% elif page_name == 'security' %}

Security

We take the security of your anonymous data seriously. Our architecture is designed to minimize risk at every level.

Encryption

All traffic between your device and Krine is encrypted using industry-standard TLS.

Minimal Logging

We configure our servers to discard IP logs after a short rotation period and do not store IP addresses alongside posts.

{% elif page_name == 'safety' %}

Safety Center

Krine is built for vulnerability, which requires a foundation of safety. The live service combined automated tools with community reporting.

Crisis Resources

Krine is not a replacement for professional help. If you or someone you know is in immediate danger or severe distress, please contact local emergency services immediately.
