About Krine

Krine is a safe haven for anonymous words. It exists as a thought experiment due to our key belief that honesty thrives when identity is removed from the equation entirely. Our platform is designed to let you express your true thoughts without the pressure of a persona or a mask.

Built with privacy at its very core, Krine uses advanced moderation to keep the community safe while maintaining your anonymity. Write whatever comes to mind and see what others write.
